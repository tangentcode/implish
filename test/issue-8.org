* Issue #8 follow-up: ',' separates arguments; only ; | newline end an expression

A comma after a function value passes it arguments (as on main), or
threads a value into a verb. It never ends an expression, and comma
threading and infix/postfix verbs never cross ; | or newline.

** TEST verb-after-sep : a verb right after ; is not given the separator as an argument
#+begin_src
> 1 2 3; rev 4 5 6
6 5 4
> f: {x + 1}

> f: {x + 1}, 2; f 5

> 1 2 3; rev

> 2; f

#+end_src

=rev[]= and ={x + 1}[]= are how a bare verb shows (same as typing =rev=
alone); before the fix these were parsed as =rev[;]= and =f[;]=.
