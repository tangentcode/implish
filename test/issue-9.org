Regression tests for tangentcode/implish#9. Run with =npm test=.

* Issue #9: evaluator state is restored when a function body throws

** TEST throw-restores-x : x is restored after a failing call
#+begin_src
> x: 10
10
> f: {x + bad}
{x + bad}
> f[99]
Error: undefined word: bad
> x
10
#+end_src

** TEST throw-restores-xyz : x, y and z are all restored
#+begin_src
> x: 10  y: 20  z: 30
30
> g: {x + y + z + bad}
{x + y + z + bad}
> g[1; 2; 3]
Error: undefined word: bad
> x
10
> y
20
> z
30
#+end_src
